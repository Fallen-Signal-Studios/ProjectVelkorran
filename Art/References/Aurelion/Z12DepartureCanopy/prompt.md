# Z12 departure canopy visual reference

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 23 September 2026 as a concept
reference, before any new Z12 canopy architecture is authored. The image is
`Z12-Departure-Canopy-Concept.png`. It is not an Unreal asset or dimensional
authority.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: art-direction reference for a modular
Unreal Engine 5.7 environment kit for the M13 Z12 departure dock.

Production prompt:

> A production-quality, highly detailed cinematic environment concept of one
> ancient Aurelion open dock canopy, to guide a custom Blender kit and
> image-guided 3D previsualization. This is the quiet departure after Tarrik
> and Selene voluntarily agree to stay together briefly, then separate under
> political summons. No character portrait or action scene. A 28 m by 18 m
> scenic berth extends from a 42 m by 24 m playable concourse. Camera at human
> eye height from the concourse threshold toward the empty berth and open
> cosmic sky. Keep a straight 5 m clear walking/camera lane to the established
> shuttle boarding position, with canopy supports outside it. A distant
> damaged fleet and sealed luminous Wound remain background silhouettes.
> Pre-Dominion, pre-Reformation Sovereign-era star-containment architecture:
> monumental ivory ceramic-metal ribs with carved stepped profiles, deep
> recessed coffers, restrained gold registration inlays, dark steel joints,
> and suspended white-gold energy conduits. Subtle five-point Crownmark
> structural geometry. Show repeated 4 m rib bays, a special end bay, detailed
> support feet and separated high roof cassettes. Rich machining, subtle edge
> wear and soot, plausible joints, layered depth. Cold blue-black aftermath
> twilight, controlled white-gold practical light, distant warm Dominion and
> cool Reformation reflections, strong silhouette and contact shadows. Wide
> 16:9 player-eye view with the full crown and support bases in frame. Avoid a
> generic spaceship corridor, airport, fantasy temple, smooth low-poly slabs,
> UI/text, blocked center lane, excessive bloom, logos and watermarks.

Design sources: the August 14 Origins TDD's M12–M13 lower-Aurelion slice;
`Docs/AurelionLayoutContract-2026-09-07.md` Z12 envelope, dock and lane
dimensions; Origins manuscript chapter 26 (PDF pages 510–511), in which the
protagonists' voluntary conversation precedes separate summons. The latter
informs the restrained aftermath mood, not new level topology.

The image includes a plausible material/detail direction and an open sky
silhouette, but the apparent distances, bay counts, sky, ships and floor are
interpretive. The current M13 map, its existing opposite berths, shuttle
anchors, native collision, navigation and cinematics remain authoritative.

The image was attached to the [image-guided Higgsfield Z12 canopy study](https://higgsfield.ai/3d-jutsu/d06c9c1f-d8c4-470e-8439-18cfa1d9df2f),
created as `M13 Z12 Aurelion Departure Canopy Previs`. Its brief requests one
separable, noninteractive canopy study with a 5 m clear center lane, not a
replacement room. On 23 September the camera view still showed primitive
blocks after 29 minutes, while the scene reported ignored invalid shared edits.
The generation was stopped and rejected as an Unreal asset source. No export or
Unreal import was made. Future architecture work uses the custom Blender kit.
