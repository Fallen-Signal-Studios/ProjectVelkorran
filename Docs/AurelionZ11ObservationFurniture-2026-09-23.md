# Aurelion M13 Z11 observation furniture

The [Z11 observation-gallery reference](ArtReferences/AurelionArchitecture-2026-09-23/Z11-Observation-Gallery.png) was generated from the 7 September level layout (Z11, 24 × 16 m) and *Origins* chapter 26, pages 507–508. It calls for one dark physical table, six age-polished chairs, and a quiet view with the red remnant left, white remnant right, and the Wound between them. This pass replaces the repeated visible vendor furniture with one custom table and six matching chairs. It does not change the authored route or interaction collision.

The editable Blender scene, two module FBXs, studio image, manifest, geometry verification, and measured pre-change map baseline are under [`Art/Source/Aurelion/Z11ObservationFurniture`](../Art/Source/Aurelion/Z11ObservationFurniture). The generation script is [`build_z11_observation_furniture.py`](../Art/Source/Aurelion/build_z11_observation_furniture.py). The modules have two UV layers and no authored collision. The existing hidden table and six chair seat/back pairs remain the sole physical collision owners. The twelve vendor mesh instances remain at their original transforms as hidden fallback geometry.

The fit and fresh-load verification scripts are [`fit_m13_observation_furniture.py`](../Scripts/Editor/fit_m13_observation_furniture.py) and [`verify_m13_observation_furniture.py`](../Scripts/Editor/verify_m13_observation_furniture.py). The reviewed, saved in-engine evidence is in `Saved/Validation/Aurelion/Z11FurnitureSaved-20260923-103110-5c11240a/`; the independent portable reload check is in `Z11FurniturePortableVerify-20260923-103919-e05d368f/`. That check passed with seven custom visuals, twelve hidden vendor instances, thirteen unchanged native collision actors, no dirty map packages, and an unchanged M12 map hash. Blender's clean FBX roundtrip reported `ARCHITECTURE_KIT_FBX_ROUNDTRIP_PASS`.

An earned CP9 checkpoint load was also exercised twice in UE 5.7 after the map save (`Saved/Validation/Aurelion/Z11FurnitureCP9Reload-20260923-103530-58837b9a/`). The report status is `passed_requires_visual_review`: both loads restored the earned journal, evidence, inventory, companion identity and position, HUD, and input; both maps remained unchanged during the test. This is a route-state regression, not a full Z11 player traversal.

The chapter 26 window composition was subsequently given a [framed exterior vista](AurelionZ11ObservationVista-2026-09-23.md). Existing white wall panels and scene-request consoles still keep the room short of the black-stone, intimate concept; they need an architectural surface and interaction-prop pass before claiming full visual alignment.
