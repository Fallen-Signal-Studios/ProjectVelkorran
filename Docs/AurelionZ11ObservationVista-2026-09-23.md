# M13 Z11 observation vista

The Z11 window originally showed an almost empty star field, so the red remnant, white remnant and Wound described in *Origins* chapter 26 were not readable. The [architecture reference](ArtReferences/AurelionArchitecture-2026-09-23/Z11-Observation-Gallery.png) established their left/center/right relationship before this map change.

An exterior-only matte plate was generated from that reference with the built-in imagegen tool. Its [source PNG and exact prompt](../Art/Source/Aurelion/Z11ObservationVista/prompt.md) are retained. A dedicated unlit, two-sided material renders the plate just outside the existing Z11 window. The material's UV orientation puts the red remnant on the viewer's left, the black Wound at center, and the smaller white remnant on the right. The plane has no collision, shadow casting, or navigation influence. Existing room geometry, railings, sky, and mission actors are untouched.

The [before](Validation/AurelionZ11ObservationVista-2026-09-23/before.png) and [after](Validation/AurelionZ11ObservationVista-2026-09-23/after.png) images are 1600 × 900 fixed game-view captures taken in UE 5.7 from the same player-height Z11 camera. The plate was saved only after the unsaved preview made all three celestial elements legible. Save/reload evidence is under `Saved/Validation/Aurelion/Z11VistaSaved-20260923-105514-dd1ea256/`. Fresh-load checks under `Z11VistaFreshVerify2-20260923-110026-576afb1a/` passed: one vista actor, expected texture/material and UV rotation, zero collision/nav influence, furniture fit retained, no dirty map packages, and M12 unchanged. The earned CP9 checkpoint roundtrip under `Z11VistaCP9Reload-20260923-110151-930c3ad8/` passed twice with unchanged maps and restored journal, evidence, inventory, companion, HUD, and input.

This is a framed vista, not a full 360-degree exterior. From far side angles the finite plate edge can appear beyond the room's wall. The current white wall panels and scene-request consoles also remain more utilitarian than the chapter 26 concept. A later architectural and prop pass should address those, followed by a player-controlled Z11 traversal and packaged performance review.
