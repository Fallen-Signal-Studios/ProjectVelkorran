These facts should use the existing campaign journal, evidence custody and cinematic/handoff mechanisms. The package does not introduce a second save system or a new universal terminal authority. Placeholder scene identifiers are dependency contracts, not proof that a scene exists or has played.

## Gameplay adaptation decisions

The TDD's mixed-survivor protection/formation encounter and Selene sensor/command-network route are gameplay adaptations. The manuscript's lower-junction rescue, freeze/break cooperation and impromptu ceasefire support their intent; their exact objective sequence is not a verbatim manuscript scene.

The first preparation target is a bounded Tarrik encounter with stable hostile and protected participant identities. The encounter owner captures the entry checkpoint, controls attempts and restores participants. Only a successful current encounter can grant its corresponding objective. Required survivors belong to that same checkpoint. A survivor loss fails the attempt; retry restores the entry conditions. Mandatory story continuation is not permanently deleted by one failed combat attempt.

Enemy roles and formation composition remain authored content. Existing Hound/Handler assets may be useful mechanical proxies, but they do not establish the final Aurelion faction roster, Eclipse presentation or encounter tuning. Do not rename a prototype role and call the TDD roster qualified.

The proposed local decision is which vulnerable group receives immediate protection. It is an adaptation candidate, not a newly asserted manuscript event. Each option needs an observable escape consequence and a later dialogue consumer, and both must rejoin the fixed containment/evidence spine. Canonical scene contracts must not silently pick this option on the creator's behalf; initial prep can retain the dependency without implementing its final staging.

## Morning qualification sequence

1. Compile the exact branch in the complete UE 5.7 work project and run the complete source-discovered native selection. The earlier 424-pass result belongs to the baseline and does not qualify these changes.
2. Inspect the authoring manifest and supply real assets/placed participants. Keep missing dependencies explicit. Run source-preserving setup with PIE stopped, then fresh-load the authored output.
3. Prove ordinary input, perception and hostility on a cold encounter start. Record the AI timeline before initialization; do not infer absent events from an observer attached late.
4. Exercise hostile victory, protected-participant death, player death, retry, late callbacks, save/load, and repeated completion. Require one durable success fact for one successful encounter.
5. Add Selene's real sensor/command encounter, controlled handoffs, companion setup, cinematic proof, the local consequence and aftermath in subsequent authoring passes. Test each added boundary before extending the route.
6. Cook and play the authored route with physical keyboard/controller input. Preserve whole-campaign preflight failures until fixed; a selected-map cook is not a full-campaign shipping pass.

The final slice gate still requires the TDD's player-research thresholds, checkpoint/reload success above 99.5% in automation/soak, representative performance captures and measured production cost. This source package cannot replace those gates.
