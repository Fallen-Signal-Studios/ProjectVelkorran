# M13 Z12 open berth vistas — 23 September 2026

The final Higgsfield blockout did not meet the production-mesh bar, so this pass used no further Higgsfield generations. A new [open-vista reference](../ArtReferences/AurelionArchitecture-2026-09-23/Z12-Open-Berth-Vistas.png) preceded modeling. It follows the August TDD, the measured M13 berths and chapter 26's red remnant / closed Wound / white remnant relationship. The earlier solid rear-bulkhead placement was rejected because it blocked that view.

[The editable Blender source](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/build_z12_open_vista_kit.py) produces two 4.45 × 3.96 m-footprint, 23.5/18.5 m tapering black-stone spires with ivory collars and keyed bridge sockets, plus a 12.39 m segmented flying bridge. The `.blend`, three FBXs, manifest and [round-trip verifier](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/verify_z12_open_vista_kit.py) are in [Z12OpenVistaKit](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/Z12OpenVistaKit/). Clean Blender 4.5 FBX reimport passed dimensions, two UV channels, material slots, finite/nondegenerate geometry and 7,256 / 7,256 / 21,432 triangle counts. The three owned Unreal static meshes use the existing Aurelion kit materials, Nanite and no simple collision. `Z12OpenVistaFreshAssets-20260923-221616-28ee408d` independently reopened all three in UE 5.7 and passed source-FBX path, bounds, two UVs, material slots, Nanite and zero simple/convex collision with both maps unchanged. These are scenic candidates, not a traversable bridge or new route. An in-engine preview moved them toward both berth windows, but they still read mostly as narrow dark posts behind the shuttles and canopy. **No spire or bridge actor was saved to M13.** They need a better player-eye composition and surface finish before placement or 90% visual-alignment credit.

Two much simpler remnant plates were reviewed and saved behind the west/east berth glazing: `Aurelion_Z12_Dominion_RemnantPlate` at X −8700 and `Aurelion_Z12_Reformation_RemnantPlate` at X +8700, both Y 47500. Their owned materials crop the existing Z11 remnant image differently; they do not alter the source texture. Both actors have collision and navigation influence disabled. The [west window capture](AurelionZ12OpenVista-2026-09-23/dominion-window.png) visibly picks up the red remnant around the Dominion shuttle; the [east window capture](AurelionZ12OpenVista-2026-09-23/reformation-window.png) picks up a colder white band around the Reformation shuttle. The canopy and mullions still obscure much of each vista, and the ships remain short of hero quality. These captures are an incremental scenic improvement, not final art acceptance.

The next art gate is to recompose the custom spires/bridge and improve the shuttle/foreground treatment at a normal player camera, then run a representative movement and performance check before saving any additional actors. This pass remains incremental toward the requested 90% TDD alignment; it does not establish that threshold.

A later [six-frame berth composition comparison](AurelionZ12BerthSightline-2026-09-23.md) rejected simple shuttle recession and nose-out rotation: they expose a little more remnant color but shrink or flatten the ship silhouettes. No ship transform was saved. The aperture/mullion and canopy need the next architectural revision before the unused open-vista meshes can be judged in a meaningful exterior view.
