# M13 Z12 concourse ceiling — 23 September 2026

The 42 × 24 m departure concourse now has a complete custom Aurelion roof instead of a short generic strip and exposed star field. [Before](AurelionZ12ConcourseCeiling-2026-09-23/before-east.png), [saved east view](AurelionZ12ConcourseCeiling-2026-09-23/center-east.png), [saved west view](AurelionZ12ConcourseCeiling-2026-09-23/center-west.png), and [upward detail](AurelionZ12ConcourseCeiling-2026-09-23/center-up.png) are fixed Unreal editor game-view captures. The [ceiling reference](../ArtReferences/AurelionArchitecture-2026-09-23/Z12-Concourse-Ceiling.png) was generated before modeling from the August TDD, September layout and Origins chapter-26 departure setting.

A read-only M13 audit `Z12CeilingClusterAudit-20260923-191851-33650c39/z12-ceiling-audit.json` found 22 noncolliding `SM_Scifi_Floor_04` instances at Z≈604.6 cm, confined to Y 46720–47140 cm. No comparable roof art covered the rest of the room. The hidden native walls and lintel retained their QueryAndPhysics collision. This is the actual source of the star-field gap, not an exposure setting.

The editable [Blender source](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/Z12ConcourseCeiling/Aurelion-Z12-Concourse-Ceiling.blend) and [builder](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/build_z12_concourse_ceiling.py) make two alternating 6 × 6 × 0.8 m bay variants. Each has 18,612 triangles, two UV channels, a closed roof back, layered ivory half-beams, deep black-stone coffer, physical gold contacts and a small luminous service lens. The 7 × 4 layout places its lowest soffit at 5.6 m above the route; the two variants alternate without putting a beam across the 4.6 m high dock-window aperture. Separate FBXs passed a [fresh Blender round trip](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/Z12ConcourseCeiling/verification.json). The [source render](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/Z12ConcourseCeiling/ceiling-source.png) is an asset study, not the live game view.

The [unsaved Unreal preview](../../Scripts/Editor/preview_m13_z12_concourse_ceiling.py) replaced only those 22 visual instances in memory with 28 visual-only static mesh actors. It recorded unchanged M12/M13 map hashes, all other actors' transforms and collision, and NoCollision/no-navigation settings for the new bays. After inspecting the preview, the [guarded save](../../Scripts/Editor/save_m13_z12_concourse_ceiling.py) checked its FBX hashes and exact map hash, backed up M13, saved and reloaded the map. The reload verified all 28 mesh identities and positions, retired-panel count zero, NoCollision/no-navigation, unchanged other actors and unchanged M12 map. Its report is `Saved/Validation/Aurelion/Z12CeilingSaved-20260923-193326-726ee49e/z12-ceiling-save.json`; M13 moved from SHA-256 `2ec45657…72f4ea` to `ce18e403…27250`.

The editor captures show the roof meets the walls and both scenic dock views remain open. The panels are more authored than the graybox strip, but the repeated bay rhythm, floor/furniture finish, shuttle forms, cinematic light grading and target-hardware performance are not final. The map needs rendered player movement and uninterrupted M12→M13 regression before the mission or visual target can be declared complete.

The saved map was also checked with two public earned CP9 loads from M12 in `Z12CeilingCP9Roundtrip-20260923-193552-9324e43b`. Both loaded the authored M13 state in new PIE worlds, kept the map files unchanged, released player input, and showed one Tarrik HUD at 100 health after about four seconds of settlement. The probe status was `passed_requires_visual_review`, and I inspected its first [rendered player frame](AurelionZ12ConcourseCeiling-2026-09-23/cp9-reload-0.png). It shows the roof edge in the ordinary departure view without hiding the HUD or eastern berth. The fixed upward editor views provide more direct ceiling evidence; neither check establishes moving-camera or target-hardware performance quality.
