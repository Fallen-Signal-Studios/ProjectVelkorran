# Z12 flanking-spire sightline check — 24 September 2026

The [open-berth design reference](../ArtReferences/AurelionArchitecture-2026-09-23/Z12-Open-Berth-Vistas.png) shows tall Aurelion structure framing distinct Dominion and Reformation shuttles against red and white remnant views. Earlier Unreal tests put the Blender-authored Z12 spires directly behind the hulls, where the shuttle and canopy obscured them. This check moved the same verified, already imported custom meshes to the aft and fore sides of both berths, outside the occupied 18 m shuttle footprint.

`Z12FlankingSpirePreview-20260924-085826-a99ad2e2` loaded the current M13 map in UE 5.7. The [unsaved preview script](../../Scripts/Editor/preview_m13_flanking_vista_spires.py) placed four no-collision, no-navigation spires at X ±55.5 m, Y 462.5/487.5 m and captured 80° fixed cameras at normal player height. It preserved all pre-existing actors and did not save either mission map. The [west](AurelionZ12FlankingSpire-2026-09-24/west.png) and [east](AurelionZ12FlankingSpire-2026-09-24/east.png) route frames, plus [west close](AurelionZ12FlankingSpire-2026-09-24/west-close.png) and [east close](AurelionZ12FlankingSpire-2026-09-24/east-close.png), were visually reviewed against the saved upper-sidelight images.

The spires appear only as nearly black vertical posts at the sides of the aperture. Their modeled collars and sockets do not read at player height, and the arrangement does not solve the low opening, central mullion, or canopy occlusion. **Placement rejected; no spire actor or bridge was saved to M13.** Both existing faction vistas and ships remain as authored. The useful next geometry change is a redesigned player-facing aperture/canopy that actually exposes a larger upper exterior field, followed by a new fixed-height Unreal preview before saving. Simply adding more distant spires is unlikely to reach the requested visual quality.

This is a measured art rejection, not gameplay, packaged-performance, or 90% visual-alignment acceptance.
