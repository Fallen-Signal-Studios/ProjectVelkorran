# Z12 sovereign oculus ceiling — 24 September 2026

The [Z12 concourse ceiling study](../ArtReferences/AurelionArchitecture-2026-09-23/Z12-Concourse-Ceiling.png), August TDD §11.3 and the measured M13 42 × 24 m concourse guided a custom 6 m visual ceiling module. The [editable Blender builder](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/build_z12_concourse_oculus.py) makes four inset black-stone cassettes, layered ivory ribs, machined gold circuits and a central luminous oculus. Its `.blend`, FBX, render and manifest are in [Z12ConcourseOculus](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/Z12ConcourseOculus/). The asset is an authored model, not a texture overlay or a change to gameplay collision.

Blender 4.5 FBX round trip passed: 5.99 × 5.99 × 0.777 m, 34,464 triangles, two UV channels, five materials, zero degenerate faces and no collision hulls. A first unsaved Unreal preview put it over the two center bays, but the fixed player-height cameras saw the next bays instead. A corrected preview (`Z12OculusApproachPreview-20260924-000639-df0e72aa`) put it over the Dominion approach at X −600, Y 47200 and Reformation approach at X +600, Y 47800, both at Z 560 cm. The motif reads distinctly in the [Dominion before](AurelionZ12SovereignOculus-2026-09-24/dominion-before.png) / [after](AurelionZ12SovereignOculus-2026-09-24/dominion-after.png) and [Reformation before](AurelionZ12SovereignOculus-2026-09-24/reformation-before.png) / [after](AurelionZ12SovereignOculus-2026-09-24/reformation-after.png) fixed camera pairs. It remains subtle during level-ground navigation; the captured comparison intentionally tilts up 30° to inspect the ceiling.

The reviewed replacement saved only two noncolliding roof visuals in `L_Aurelion_M13` and left the old visual tile components hidden for reversible review. Native wall collision, navigation, the M12 map and all unrelated actors were preserved. The final actors are `Aurelion_Z12_Dominion_SovereignOculus` and `Aurelion_Z12_Reformation_SovereignOculus`. A fresh UE 5.7 editor load (`Z12OculusFreshVerify-20260924-001811-d38fc42c`) checked their source FBX path, material slots, two UVs, zero simple/convex collision, exact transforms and Z bounds, hidden old tiles and unchanged map files. M13 SHA-256 moved from `63b3ee34…94b3814b` to `32d6fcaf…df576e`; the pre-save map is backed up under `Saved/Validation/Aurelion/Z12OculusSaved-20260924-001104-d03f9b66`.
