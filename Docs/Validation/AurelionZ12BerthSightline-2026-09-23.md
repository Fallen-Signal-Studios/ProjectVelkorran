# M13 berth sightline comparison — 23 September 2026

The [open-berth reference](../ArtReferences/AurelionArchitecture-2026-09-23/Z12-Open-Berth-Vistas.png) calls for the red Dominion remnant and white Reformation remnant to read beyond sparse ancient structures and separate shuttles. The saved 8 × 6 m side view modules, dock canopy and shuttle silhouettes are much tighter than that painting. This pass tested whether a simple scenic ship reposition could recover the vista before revising more architecture.

`Z12BerthComposition-20260923-232942-7a6dc2d5` ran in visible UE 5.7 M13 with Aura disabled. The [unsaved comparison script](../../Scripts/Editor/preview_m13_berth_sightline.py) captured the current full-size shuttles, a 0.82-scale recessed placement within both 28 × 18 m dock envelopes, and an equally scaled nose-out placement. It changed only the two noncolliding scenic ship transforms in editor memory, then restored both. It did not save assets or maps. M12 and M13 SHA-256 values stayed `64a4517b…2b844a4` and `63b3ee34…94b3814b`.

The recessed trial opens a noticeable red band over the Dominion ship and more pale background over the Reformation ship, but the ships become too small and low in the opening. Nose-out improves red-space exposure yet reduces the Reformation craft to a dark end-on shape; those two additional frames remain in the ignored local run directory. Neither trial approaches the reference's high, open exterior composition, so **no shuttle transform was saved**. The dominant blockers are the central armored mullion, low aperture and roof/canopy sightline. A later custom architectural viewport/canopy revision should create a deliberate upper vista while preserving native wall collision, the authored 28 × 18 m dock envelopes, the distinct landed craft and the clear playable concourse. It needs a new player-height preview before a guarded map save.

A new [berth clerestory concept](../ArtReferences/AurelionArchitecture-2026-09-23/Z12-Berth-Clerestory-Concept.png) illustrates that direction against the actual west capture: keep the ship large in the lower view and add a structurally framed upper band for the red remnant and sparse spires. Its upper aperture is aspirational. A fresh UE roof audit (`Z12CurrentClerestoryAudit-20260923-233859-28b12eee`) measured the 7 × 4 roof grid at Z 560–640 cm and the native side-wall collision up to Z 600 cm. The eight outer roof bays are visual-only. A clean Blender 4.5 FBX round trip produced a 6 × 6 m raised edge candidate; the unsaved UE preview `Z12ClerestoryPreview-20260923-235103-38da94a1` fitted it over those eight bays without changing wall collision. [West](AurelionZ12BerthSightline-2026-09-23/clerestory-west-rejected.png) and [east](AurelionZ12BerthSightline-2026-09-23/clerestory-east-rejected.png) eye-height views show a dark band above the windows, not a useful remnant vista. The sloped solid roof and current canopy obscure the intended upper opening. **The roof candidate was rejected; no roof or shuttle changes were saved.** The imported prototype mesh is not part of the shipped map.

This is a visual comparison, not a gameplay or performance qualification. The source image and six editor frames were inspected; the four linked frames are retained for reviewer comparison.
