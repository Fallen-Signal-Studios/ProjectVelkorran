# Aurelion Z12 armored view kit — 23 September 2026

The M13 departure concourse now has two custom 8 × 6 m framed and glazed views onto the existing western Dominion and eastern Reformation docks. This is an incremental replacement of the central graybox side-wall visuals, not a finished Z12 art pass. [East in-engine capture](AurelionZ12ArmoredViews-2026-09-23/east-view-after.png), [west in-engine capture](AurelionZ12ArmoredViews-2026-09-23/west-view-after.png), and [earned CP9 PIE capture](AurelionZ12ArmoredViews-2026-09-23/cp9-reload-0.png) record the current appearance.

The design preceded the geometry: [departure-concourse study](../ArtReferences/AurelionArchitecture-2026-09-23/Z12-Departure-Concourse.png) and [armored-view module study](../ArtReferences/AurelionArchitecture-2026-09-23/Z12-Armored-View-Module.png). The 7 September layout calls Z12 a 42 × 24 m common departure concourse with separate western and eastern berths. Origins chapter 26 places the red remnant to one side, the smaller hard white remnant to the other, and the closed Wound between them. The module's 8 m visual bay and 6.4 × 4.6 m clear aperture were chosen around existing 4 m coffer spacing and the central native rib. The studies are art direction, not measured map evidence.

The editable [Blender source](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/Z12ArmoredView/Aurelion-Z12-Armored-View.blend) and [builder](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/build_z12_armored_view.py) produce separate frame, translucent pane and center-mullion FBXs. The frame has 43,240 triangles, the pane 376, and the mullion 10,716; each has two UV channels. The frame and mullion use Nanite; translucent glass does not. A fresh Blender export/import check [passed](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/Z12ArmoredView/verification.json). The source render is [here](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/Z12ArmoredView/armored-view-source.png).

The M13 fit audit identified 16 central coffer instances and two noncolliding vendor columns that covered the dock views. The [guarded editor script](../../Scripts/Editor/preview_m13_armored_view.py) removed only those visuals, placed six custom static-mesh actors, saved M13 and reloaded it. It verified the 32 remaining coffer transforms, absence of the two vendor visuals, all six new actors' NoCollision/no-navigation settings, and unchanged hidden native wall and rib QueryAndPhysics collision. The prior M13 map was copied to the ignored validation run before the save. M12's on-disk map hash remained `64a4517b…b844a4`; saved M13 moved from `74db4902…d1bac8a` to `588d13a4…76e5e9a`. The full machine-readable result is `Saved/Validation/Aurelion/M13ArmoredViewSaved-20260923-183425-38fa1023/armored-view-preview.json`.

The earned M12→M13 CP9 continuation was then loaded twice in a fresh UE editor process against the saved M13 map. Both public checkpoint loads recovered the authored M13 state, Tarrik, an unpaused world, one visible HUD and released input. The probe reported `passed_requires_visual_review`, requested load count 2 and unchanged M12/M13 map files: `Saved/Validation/Aurelion/M13ArmoredViewCP9Roundtrip-20260923-183630-73b5d7d7/earned-m13-reload.json`. The first visual capture was reviewed. This tests continuation and reload, not traversal through every approach or performance on target hardware.

Visual limitations remain obvious: the docks and glazing are underlit, the distant remnant/Wound composition is absent from this live camera, and the surrounding ceiling, floor, furniture and exit dressing are still sparse. The new frames are more specific than the graybox panels but the current view does not reach the image studies' material richness or the requested 90% TDD visual alignment. Further work should address those surroundings and lighting with in-engine player-eye checks before claiming final art quality.
