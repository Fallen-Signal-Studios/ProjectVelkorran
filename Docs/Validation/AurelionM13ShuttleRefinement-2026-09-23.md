# M13 departure shuttle refinement — 23 September 2026

The final Higgsfield Z08 GLB failed the source-quality gate documented in [its review](HiggsfieldFirstBlockout-2026-09-23.md), so no further Higgsfield generation was used. The next visual pass was authored in Blender and checked in the saved UE 5.7 M13 map. The [paired faction shuttle reference](../ArtReferences/AurelionArchitecture-2026-09-23/Z12-Departure-Shuttles-Detail.png) preceded modeling; it follows the August faction direction, September's 28 × 18 m scenic berths, and the chapter-26 separate departures. Its visible boarding ramps/interiors are illustrative, not a new gameplay feature.

The first-pass dock shuttles had broad flat prows and large unbroken hull panels. The revised editable [Blender builder](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/build_departure_shuttles.py) keeps the measured hull and berth envelopes while adding two UV channels, tapered noses, visible forward glazing, inset sensor and nacelle faces, cassettes, registers, hatch hardware, collars, thruster vanes and layered landing gear. The source FBXs are 37,884 Dominion and 38,748 Reformation triangles, 16.60 × 14.73 × 4.93 m and 15.39 × 14.15 × 4.63 m respectively; the [fresh FBX round trip](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/departure-shuttle-verification.json) checks dimensions, five material slots, two UV channels and triangle counts. Both remain no-collision, static scenic craft; no boarding, flight or gameplay actor changed.

`M13ShuttleProwPreview-20260923-202133-ede9c878` reimported only the two owned static meshes. It verified preserved material bindings, two UV channels, no collision, unchanged actor transforms/collision, and unchanged M12/M13 map bytes. Unreal marked the loaded M13 package dirty while refreshing mesh render state; the isolated editor closed without saving it. A subsequent guarded [palette pass](../../Scripts/Editor/refine_m13_shuttle_armor_materials.py) changed only the two shuttle-only armor material constants from the original bright red/blue to the darker source oxblood/navy values and increased roughness from 0.32 to 0.39. Its asset-referencer check found only each matching shuttle. M12 and M13 map SHA-256 stayed `64a4517b…2b844a4` and `ce18e403…27250`.

The final editor captures are [Dominion from the concourse](AurelionM13ShuttleRefinement-2026-09-23/dominion-close.png), [Reformation from the concourse](AurelionM13ShuttleRefinement-2026-09-23/reformation-close.png), and the [Dominion](AurelionM13ShuttleRefinement-2026-09-23/dominion-dock.png) / [Reformation](AurelionM13ShuttleRefinement-2026-09-23/reformation-dock.png) dock-level checks. The tapered fronts and faction silhouettes now read better at dock level. At the actual concourse, the protected central mullion and berth distance hide most microdetail; the hulls still read as fairly simple forms. Surface texture, material breakup, near-player composition, scene grading and target-hardware performance need more work. These assets are an incremental improvement, not AAA hero-asset or 90% visual acceptance.
