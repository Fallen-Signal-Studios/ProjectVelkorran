# Z12 berth bulkhead source and Unreal review — 23 September 2026

The final Higgsfield blockout was rejected in the [earlier review](HiggsfieldFirstBlockout-2026-09-23.md); this is a custom Blender/Unreal pass. A new [bulkhead reference](../ArtReferences/AurelionArchitecture-2026-09-23/Z12-Berth-Bulkhead.png) was generated before modeling from the August ivory/black/gold direction, the measured 28 × 18 m M13 berth and the established departure art. It is a modelable wall study, not a revised story layout.

The editable [Blender builder](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/build_z12_berth_bulkhead.py) exports Plain and Service 4.15 m × 7.57 m bays. Their layered stone bearings, dark-steel equipment cassettes, restrained conductors, service register, coffer joint and small lens contacts are meant to fit the canopy rib rhythm. The source is under [Z12BerthBulkhead](../../Art/Source/Aurelion/Z12BerthBulkhead). Clean-process FBX import verified both meshes, two UV channels, material identities, dimensions, finite geometry, no degenerate faces and no authored collision: 13,536 / 22,124 triangles. The two owned static meshes imported into Unreal with the existing Aurelion kit materials, two UVs, Nanite and no simple collision.

`Z12BerthFreshAssets-20260923-212856-4fdf3217` reopened the imported assets in a new UE 5.7 process and verified their exact source FBX paths, 415 × 78.15 × 757 cm bounds, two UV channels, material slots, enabled Nanite and zero simple/convex collision. The editor reported a clean map and unchanged map hashes.

Three unsaved UE 5.7 M13 previews tested rear bays, side returns and then four shadowless 1,800-lumen wall washes. `Z12BerthBulkheadPreview-20260923-211307-19a16d41` confirmed the first rear fit. `Z12BerthReturnsPreview-20260923-211644-781c6a30` closed side gaps but was too dark. `Z12BerthLitPreview-20260923-211952-38d8561d` made the silhouette readable in the [player-height concourse view](AurelionZ12BerthBulkheadPrototype-2026-09-23/dominion-player-view.png) and exposed the geometry in an [oblique dock view](AurelionZ12BerthBulkheadPrototype-2026-09-23/dominion-oblique.png). Every preview preserved pre-existing actor transforms and collision, used NoCollision/no-navigation placements, and left both map files unchanged. The M13 map remained SHA-256 `ce18e4035a2ce6726d2970c1669a3255937ec7327b918aba06aef0187dc27250`.

**Placement decision:** do not save the full rear-wall assembly. It closes the open remnant/Wound vista established by the departure concept and manuscript, and from the normal concourse view most of its detail is hidden behind the shuttle and central mullion. The brighter oblique capture also reads too flat for final AAA acceptance. The meshes remain reusable candidates for dock-side service returns or another protected interior, but the M13 map placement and four experimental lights are rejected. Next visual work should build the actual exterior/remnant vista and a better side-return composition, then recheck from the live player and target hardware before a map save. This prototype does not advance the evidence-based visual-alignment percentage.
