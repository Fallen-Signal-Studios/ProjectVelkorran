# Echo resource and presentation contracts

Native resource work keeps Narrative attributes as the only Echo storage. There is no second meter or alternate damage pipeline. The build/automation command is in [UnrealValidation.md](UnrealValidation.md); run `ProjectVelkorran.Campaign.Echo` for the focused tests.

## Readiness and activity

Resonance remains 75. Signature resource readiness reads the currently granted Cinderline Requiem or Dispatch and delegates to the same weapon rule as activation. Requiem requires its configured wielded source; Dispatch permits a definition-granted signature without an item source because it summons Verity while another weapon is active. It uses the greater of that ability's authored minimum and cost (both native defaults are 90). Missing or removed signatures and weapon-gated signatures with an invalid wield context return no available requirement. This is a **resource** indicator; a full activation still runs the existing weapon allowlist, payload, cooldown, Busy and cancellation checks. A child with a higher minimum updates the indicator automatically. Attribute updates refresh immediately; a 0.1-second component tick also reconciles equipment/spec replication without a meter change.

A confirmed perfect deflection refreshes inactivity even at 100 Echo. Its transaction is consumed before a later spend can make it payable. Invalid/nonfinite awards, costs and checkpoint values are rejected. Explicit `BeginEncounter` and `EndEncounter` broadcast `OnEncounterScopeChanged`, resetting precision/heavy/cadence state at start, retry and end. Combat activity alone does not reset a chain.

| Tarrik perfect guard/counter | Existing Guard component keeps 12/10. | Existing guard setup. |
| Tarrik poise break | 15 from a hostile non-Echo committed result with `bPoiseBroken`. | Valid enemy poise attributes/components. |
| Tarrik heavy hits three targets | 8 once per active attack, three distinct damaged hostile targets, typed `AttackId` and heavy classification. | Primary heavy GA derives from NarrativeCombatAbility, carries `Sov.Damage.Heavy`, and uses the receipt below. |

| Selene weak point | 8 consumes the exact positive Health/Shield hit on a previously unbroken target zone, including hits below its break threshold. The separate break proof is consumed without a second award. | Authored WeakPoint zones and hit bones/materials. |
| Selene marked/exposed kill | 6 once per fatal health result when either pre-hit tag is present; both tags still give one award. | Target-owned duration GE granting `Sov.State.Target.Marked` or `Sov.State.Target.Exposed`. A cosmetic reveal alone is not exposure. |
| Selene precision chain | Each additional distinct target gives 4, at most three bonus links. First target gives only its weak-point reward. Same-target zones do not give another link. | WeakPoint setup. Prototype interval defaults to 3 seconds. |
| Selene bypass/live disable | 15 once per director attempt across gates; consumed even at cap. | Registered live threats, operational AI Sight perception, entry/exit route geometry. |
| Protection intercept | 15 from a source-owned exact committed receipt, once per threat per 5-second prototype cooldown. | Drone Gunfire's actual ray hits Tarrik while the intended focus is a live ally/civilian; a counterfactual identical ray ignoring Tarrik and his visuals must first reach that ally. Other weapons/redirects require equally verified producers. |

Accepted weak-point-hit proofs are separate from breaks, with a bounded pending queue and exact transaction/context/source/zone matching. The old WeakPointBreak reward property/getter and enum value remain for Blueprint compatibility; new feedback uses WeakPointHit. Periodic damage cannot claim precision-hit or heavy multi-target credit even when its context retains the original hit bone. All new combat awards exclude Echo-spender damage and friendly targets. Ordinary body hits grant zero and reset Selene's precision chain; incoming applied damage and encounter boundaries also reset it. Duplicate committed IDs are retained as replay fences for the component lifetime. Firing-cadence behavior remains in Tarrik's existing Cinderline component. The chosen three-link/three-second precision limits are editable prototype tuning, not additional TDD facts.

The protection receipt is native and single use. It repeats the shot's actual weapon-channel ray or sphere sweep, then repeats that same query ignoring only Tarrik and recognized character/weapon visuals. Normal shooter exclusions remain unchanged. Walls, projectiles, carried actors, and arbitrary owned/attached props still block the query; an ally-owned crate cannot impersonate that ally. Actual AI focus, living actors and team relationships are verified before damage. The result must match the exact armed effect context and committed transaction; the original weapon SourceObject and SourceAbility remain untouched. Accepted Guard and perfect defense can protect an ally with zero Health/Shield damage. Invulnerability, unrelated nested damage, stale/replayed results, and a shot aimed at Tarrik cannot award. A consumed full-meter receipt also starts the source cooldown, preventing a later spend from making it payable. See [ProtectionInterceptValidation.md](ProtectionInterceptValidation.md).

### Primary heavy Blueprint hookup

1. Use the existing native `NarrativeCombatAbility` parent and preserve calls to its activation/end lifecycle. Put `Sov.Damage.Heavy` on the heavy GA and its actual damage effect classification; do not put it on ordinary primary fire.
2. On the authoritative active ability, call `SovEchoAttackReceipt.CreateForActiveAbility(self)`. Null means no valid native activation exists. Multiple factory calls during one activation share the same ID. Retain the receipt in a Blueprint variable/UPROPERTY for a latent attack: context SourceObject is weak and UObject Outer alone does not retain the inner receipt.
3. Use that receipt as **SourceObject on the damage effect context** for all victims, preserving the real instigator, ability context, hit result, weapon-granted ability spec, damage execution and channel tags. The receipt replaces only the effect context's source object; the granted ability spec still refers to its weapon.
4. End/cancel the ability normally. A receipt cannot adopt a later activation, survive cancellation for a new damage request, or return an ID for another source. A result whose AttackId was already validated at commit can still complete its reward when a target callback ends the source ability before source notification. This producer cannot award Echo directly. Unwrapped old heavy content continues dealing damage but cannot claim the multi-target reward.

### Bypass authoring

Place `ASovEchoBypassGate`, assign its encounter director, and list stable registered threat participant IDs. Move its separate EntryVolume and ExitVolume around a real route. Volumes must not overlap. Selene must physically enter and reach the exit while the attempt remains active and every listed threat stays alive, hostile and resolvable. An actual transition from enabled to `Sov.State.Status.DeviceDisabled` while traversing may also satisfy the action. Paid Echo attacks cannot receive this generic reward while their Echo-active state is present.

Each threat's controller must have a registered `AIPerceptionComponent` with configured, enabled AI Sight. A missing/unconfigured/disabled sense fails closed. The gate reads remembered perceived actors and subscribes to perception updates throughout the route; a successful stimulus invalidates that traversal, including if sight is lost before the exit. Death, controller/perception replacement, participant replacement, time limit or attempt changes reject the receipt. The director's fixed `Selene.UndetectedBypass` attempt ledger prevents farming multiple gates or replacing the pawn to reset the cap. Retry creates a new attempt and restores the encounter's resources through the director.

No native `KnownStates` API exists in this source snapshot. This gate uses Unreal's existing perception state, not an invented parallel detection flag. Validate actual detection affiliation, sight ranges and stimuli registration in the authored map. See Epic's [AI Perception documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/ai-perception-in-unreal-engine) and [IsSenseEnabled API](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/API/Runtime/AIModule/UAIPerceptionComponent/IsSenseEnabled).

## Weapon presentation cleanup

Transforming weapons retain the existing replicated phase/serial state machine. Transition gates now release the exact cached ASC before acquiring a replacement owner's ASC, preserve other owners' tag counts, recover an externally removed effect, and coalesce reentrant tag callbacks. Losing the current character does not strand the previous gate. Character montages are stopped on the exact anim instance that played them, even after appearance/mesh replacement. Invalid duration/play-rate data cannot leave a NaN transition timer. No new equip/holster state machine was introduced.

- Portable `Scripts/Tests/EchoAwardPolicyTests.cpp` compiles against the production header and passes 22 assertions with C++17, `-Wall -Wextra -Werror -pedantic`. It covers signature boundaries, NaN/infinity, distinct-target links, cap, exact timeout boundary, clock reset, reset and invalid tuning.
- Nine Unreal runtime tests under `ProjectVelkorran.Campaign.Echo` exercise actual GAS attributes/results for readiness, cap deflection activity/replay, immutable attack receipt lifecycle, heavy target dedup, poise/command kills, Selene weak-point hits before break/marked kills and real periodic damage rejection, nested Echo spending during award callbacks, owner ASC transition cleanup, and the three protection suites described in [ProtectionInterceptValidation.md](ProtectionInterceptValidation.md).
- `Tests/Portable/SovProtectionAwardPolicyTests.cpp` passes 13 assertions against the production cooldown predicate: exact five-second boundary, same-source exclusion, clock rewind, zero/negative minimum and nonfinite input rejection.
- Unreal 5.7, UnrealHeaderTool, ZenDyn, and binary Content assets are unavailable in this environment. These native test sources have **not** been compiled or executed here. Portable tests do not substitute for engine compilation.
- Required PIE checks: live AI sight acquisition during route traversal; remembered detection; missing/disabled perception; actual disable versus dead threat; entry/exit overlap direction; attempt retry; multiple gates and protagonist replacement; precision reward UI; montage/owner replacement while drawing/stowing; dedicated-server/owning-client gate replication.
- Level content must assign the target-window GEs, primary heavy receipt hookup, registered encounter participants and route volumes. The native protection producer is limited to the verified Drone Gunfire ray; other enemy payloads do not fabricate this reward. Cinder Ward remains self-resistance rather than an ally redirect.
